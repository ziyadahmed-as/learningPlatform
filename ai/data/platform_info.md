# Fatra Academy — Platform Information

## About Us
Fatra Academy is an online learning platform that connects students with expert instructors from around the world. We serve over 45,000+ students, 2,000+ instructors, and host 12,000+ courses spanning 115 countries. Our mission is to make high-quality education accessible and engaging for everyone.

## Our Services

### 1. Video-Based Courses
Students can enroll in professionally structured, pre-recorded video courses. Each course is organized into Chapters, Lessons, and Content Blocks. Lessons include rich content types such as:
- Written text and HTML articles
- Uploaded videos and external video links
- PDF documents and handouts
- Images and reference links

Students can learn at their own pace, track their progress lesson by lesson, and earn completion certificates.

### 2. Live Stream Sessions
Instructors can host live, real-time teaching sessions with different group sizes:
- **VVIP**: Exclusive 1-on-1 private session with an instructor
- **VIP1**: Small group of up to 5 students
- **VIP2**: Group of up to 10 students
- **Normal**: Large group of up to 100 students

Live sessions include scheduled meeting times, meeting links, and support materials. Students can also rate their instructors after completing live sessions.

### 3. AI-Powered Learning Assistant
Every enrolled student has access to a personal AI tutor that understands the content of the specific course they are studying. The assistant can:
- Answer questions about lesson content
- Explain difficult concepts from the course material
- Provide summaries of key learning objectives
- Help students stay on track with their studies

### 4. AI Course Recommendations
Our platform uses advanced AI to recommend similar courses to students based on what they are already studying. If you are enrolled in a Python course, our system will suggest related programming courses that match your interests.

### 5. Instructor AI Tools
Instructors have access to AI-powered tools to help them create better content:
- **AI Course Description Generator**: Automatically generates professional, engaging course descriptions from just a title and keywords.
- **AI Curriculum Generator**: Suggests a complete chapter and lesson structure for a new course, and can auto-create the curriculum in one click.
- **Content Summarizer**: Summarizes educational content into structured bullet points for quick review.

## Pricing and Payments

### How Pricing Works
- Each course has its own price set by the instructor. Some courses are free.
- Live Stream sessions have separate pricing, starting from ETB 5,000 depending on the session type (VVIP, VIP1, VIP2, Normal).
- Payments are processed securely through Stripe.

### Instructor Earnings
- Instructors earn revenue from course enrollments and live stream sessions.
- Each instructor has a Wallet where earnings accumulate from course sales.
- Instructors can request withdrawals at any time. Withdrawal requests are reviewed and approved by the platform administration.
- Full transaction history and earnings analytics are available in the instructor dashboard.

## User Roles

### Students
- Browse and enroll in courses
- Watch video lessons and track progress
- Join live stream sessions
- Rate courses and instructors
- Get AI-powered learning assistance
- Receive personalized course recommendations
- Earn completion certificates

### Instructors
Instructors can be of three types:
- **Video Creators**: Create and publish pre-recorded video courses
- **Live Streamers**: Host live teaching sessions
- **Both**: Create courses and host live streams

To become an instructor:
1. Register on the platform
2. Complete your instructor profile (expertise, education, experience, CV, LinkedIn, portfolio)
3. Submit your profile for admin approval
4. Once approved, you can start creating courses and live sessions

### Administrators
- Manage all users on the platform
- Approve or reject instructor applications
- Approve or reject course submissions
- Oversee platform-wide analytics and statistics
- Handle withdrawal requests from instructors

## Course Lifecycle
1. **Creation**: Instructor creates a course with title, description, category, price, and thumbnail.
2. **Content Building**: Instructor adds chapters, lessons, and content blocks (text, video, PDF, etc.).
3. **Submission**: Instructor submits the course for admin review.
4. **Approval**: Admin reviews and approves (or rejects) the course.
5. **Publication**: Once approved, the course becomes visible to all students.
6. **Enrollment**: Students enroll (free or paid) and begin learning.

## Categories
Courses are organized by categories (e.g., Programming, Web Development, Data Science, Design, Business, etc.) making it easy for students to discover content that matches their interests.

## Progress Tracking
Students can track their learning progress:
- Each lesson tracks watched time and completion status
- Overall course completion percentage is calculated automatically
- Students can resume from where they left off

## Reviews and Ratings
- Students can rate and review courses they have enrolled in (1-5 stars with comments)
- Students can also rate instructors after attending live sessions
- Ratings help other students make informed choices when choosing courses

## Technical Support
If you experience any issues with the platform, our AI assistant is available 24/7 to help answer your questions. For account-related issues, please contact our support team through the platform.
